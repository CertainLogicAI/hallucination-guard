# Reply Guidelines

## Core Rules
1. **Add value or don't reply.** No "great post!", "so true!", or emoji-only replies.
2. **Match their energy.** Technical post → technical reply. Casual → casual.
3. **Under 280 chars.** Keep it tight.
4. **No links on first reply.** Build familiarity before dropping links.
5. **One reply per account per day max.** Don't stalk.

## Reply Templates by Context

### When they post about AI agents/tools
> Interesting approach. We've been building [related thing] and found [insight]. [Question that invites conversation].

### When they share a problem we solve
> We ran into the same thing. Built [tool] to handle it — [specific result]. Happy to share what worked.

### When they ask a question
> [Direct answer]. [Supporting evidence or experience]. [Optional: where to learn more].

### When they share data/research
> [Specific reaction to a data point]. This tracks with what we've seen — [your data point]. [Question or insight].

### When they announce something
> [Genuine reaction]. [How it connects to your work or audience]. [Forward-looking question].

## Anti-Patterns (never do these)
- ❌ "Check out my product!" on someone else's thread
- ❌ Replying to every post from the same person in one day
- ❌ Generic compliments ("Love this!", "So helpful!")
- ❌ Immediately pitching after someone replies to you
- ❌ Correcting someone publicly unless they're dangerously wrong
- ❌ Arguing in replies — agree to disagree and move on

## Tier-Specific Approach

### VIP (10K+ followers)
- Reply within first hour of their post (early replies get more visibility)
- Must be genuinely insightful — competition for attention is high
- One reply only — never follow up unless they respond

### HIGH (1K-10K)
- Can be slightly more conversational
- Good for building actual relationships
- Follow up if they reply to create a thread

### MEDIUM (500-1K)
- Only reply if topic is directly relevant to us
- Use these for practicing reply craft

## Measuring Success
Track weekly:
- Replies posted: ___
- Replies that got likes: ___
- Replies that got responses: ___
- New followers gained: ___
- Profile visits (check X analytics): ___
