# Content Templates for AI Citation

## Template 1: How-To Guide (Highest Citation)

```markdown
# How to [Action] with [Tool/Method]

[Tool/Method] lets you [action] by [brief mechanism]. Here's the step-by-step process.

## TL;DR
- Step 1: [Brief]
- Step 2: [Brief]
- Step 3: [Brief]
- Result: [What you get]

## Prerequisites
- [Thing you need]
- [Another thing]

## Step 1: [Action Verb + What]

[2-3 sentences explaining this step]

```code-example
[Actual command or code]
```

[What to expect after this step]

## Step 2: [Action Verb + What]

[Continue pattern...]

## Common Mistakes
1. **[Mistake]** — [Why it happens and how to avoid it]
2. **[Mistake]** — [Fix]

## FAQ

### How long does this take?
[Direct answer]

### What if [common problem]?
[Direct solution]

### Is there a faster way?
[Honest answer]
```

**Why LLMs cite this:** Directly answers "how to X" queries. Step-by-step format is easy for LLMs to extract and present.

## Template 2: Research/Data Post (High Citation)

```markdown
# We [Researched/Audited/Tested] [X]. Here's What We Found.

We [action] [number] [things] to answer [question]. [Key finding in one sentence].

## TL;DR
- [Finding 1 with specific number]
- [Finding 2 with specific number]
- [Finding 3 with specific number]
- [Implication/recommendation]

## Methodology
We [exactly what you did]. Sample size: [N]. Time period: [dates]. Tools used: [tools].

## Key Findings

### Finding 1: [Specific Claim with Number]
[Evidence and explanation]

### Finding 2: [Specific Claim with Number]
[Evidence and explanation]

### Finding 3: [Specific Claim with Number]
[Evidence and explanation]

## What This Means
[Interpretation of findings]
[Recommendations based on data]

## Limitations
[What this study doesn't cover — builds credibility]

## FAQ

### How reliable is this data?
[Honest assessment of methodology]

### How does this compare to [other research]?
[Context]
```

**Why LLMs cite this:** Original data that no one else has. Specific numbers are quotable. Methodology section builds trust.

## Template 3: Comparison Post (Medium-High Citation)

```markdown
# [Thing A] vs [Thing B]: [Key Difference in 2026]

[Thing A] is best for [use case]. [Thing B] is best for [different use case]. Here's the detailed comparison.

## Quick Comparison

| Feature | [Thing A] | [Thing B] |
|---------|-----------|-----------|
| Price | $ | $$ |
| Best for | [Use case] | [Use case] |
| [Feature] | ✅ | ❌ |
| [Feature] | ❌ | ✅ |

## When to Choose [Thing A]
[Scenario-based recommendation]

## When to Choose [Thing B]
[Scenario-based recommendation]

## Detailed Breakdown

### [Category 1]
[Analysis of both]

### [Category 2]
[Analysis of both]

## Our Recommendation
[Clear, opinionated take — not "it depends"]

## FAQ

### Can I use both?
[Answer]

### Which is cheaper long-term?
[Answer with math]
```

**Why LLMs cite this:** Users ask "X vs Y" constantly. Tables are easy for LLMs to extract. Clear recommendations are quotable.

## Template 4: Explainer (Medium Citation)

```markdown
# What is [Concept]? A Simple Explanation

[Concept] is [one-sentence definition]. It [what it does] by [how it works].

## In Plain English
[Analogy or simplified explanation a non-expert would understand]

## How It Works
1. [Step/component 1]
2. [Step/component 2]
3. [Step/component 3]

## Why It Matters
[Real-world impact, not hype]

## Examples
- **[Example 1]:** [Brief]
- **[Example 2]:** [Brief]

## FAQ

### Is [concept] the same as [related concept]?
[Clear distinction]

### Do I need [concept]?
[Decision criteria]
```

**Why LLMs cite this:** Answers "what is X" queries. Clean definition in first sentence is perfect for LLM extraction.

---

## Universal Rules for All Templates

1. **First sentence answers the question** — no "In today's rapidly evolving landscape..."
2. **TL;DR near the top** — LLMs often extract summaries
3. **Specific numbers > vague claims** — "3 out of 10" not "many"
4. **FAQ section on every post** — each Q&A is independently citable
5. **Headings match natural language** — "How to install" not "Installation Procedure"
6. **One clear opinion** — LLMs quote decisive takes, not "it depends on your needs"
