# Industry-Specific Templates

Pre-written outreach templates by vertical. Customize the bracketed fields.

## SaaS / Tech Startups

### Cold Email
```
Subject: [Their product] growth bottleneck?

Hi [Name],

[Company] is at the stage where [specific challenge: onboarding drops off, churn spikes after month 3, support tickets outpace hiring].

We helped [similar SaaS] go from [metric A] to [metric B] by [method] — took [timeframe].

Worth 10 min to compare notes?
```

### LinkedIn DM
```
Hey [Name] — saw [Company] just [trigger: launched feature, hit milestone, raised round]. Congrats.

Curious — are you seeing [common pain at their stage]? We've been working on that exact problem with [similar companies].
```

## Agency / Consulting

### Cold Email
```
Subject: Your clients' [pain point]

Hi [Name],

Quick thought: most [agency type] agencies I talk to are hemorrhaging hours on [specific task: reporting, onboarding, content creation].

We built a [solution] that [specific agency] uses to save [X hours/week] per client. They took on [Y more clients] without hiring.

60-second demo worth your time?
```

### Follow-up (Day 4)
```
Subject: Re: Your clients' [pain point]

[Name] — here's the math that convinced [reference agency]:

Before: [X hours/client/month] × [Y clients] = [Z hours wasted]
After: [reduced hours] × [same clients] = [hours saved] → [$ saved or clients gained]

Want me to run the numbers for [their agency]?
```

## Freelancer / Solopreneur

### Upwork Proposal
```
Hi — read through your project details on [specific requirement they mentioned].

I've done exactly this for [similar client]: [specific deliverable] that [measurable result].

What makes me different: [unique angle — speed, niche expertise, included extra].

Happy to start with [small milestone] at [price] so you can evaluate quality before committing to the full scope.

[Portfolio link]
```

### Cold Email to Direct Clients
```
Subject: [Their business] + [your skill]

Hi [Name],

I specialize in [specific service] for [their industry]. Noticed [specific observation about their business].

Recent example: helped [similar business] [specific result] in [timeframe]. Here's the case study: [link]

Worth a quick chat to see if there's a fit?
```

## Local Service Business

### Cold Email
```
Subject: [Business Name] — quick observation

Hi [Name],

Looked up [Business Name] online and noticed [specific issue: no online booking, reviews stopped 6 months ago, website doesn't load on mobile, Google listing missing hours].

I help [business type] in [area] fix exactly this. [Reference client] went from [X] to [Y] in [timeframe].

Want me to send a free 2-minute audit of what I'd fix first?
```

### SMS (after email, Day 7)
```
Hi [Name], this is [You] — sent you an email about [topic] for [Business Name]. Quick question: would [specific quick win] be useful? Happy to put something together, no strings.
```

## E-Commerce / DTC

### Cold Email
```
Subject: [Their brand]'s [metric] opportunity

Hi [Name],

Been following [Brand] — [genuine compliment about product/brand].

Noticed [specific opportunity: no post-purchase email flow, abandoned cart without SMS, product pages missing reviews].

For [similar brand], adding [specific tactic] drove [X% increase in metric] within [timeframe].

Want me to map out what that would look like for [Brand]?
```

### Follow-up (Day 5)
```
Subject: Re: [Their brand]'s [metric] opportunity

[Name] — one quick data point:

[Industry stat relevant to the opportunity you identified].

[Brand] is leaving that on the table right now. Happy to show you the quick fix — takes [timeframe] to implement.
```

## B2B Enterprise

### Cold Email (to exec)
```
Subject: [Their company] + [outcome]

[Name],

[One sentence: specific observation about their company or industry].

We work with [2-3 recognizable companies in their space] on [specific outcome]. Typical result: [metric].

Worth a 15-minute call next week?

[Signature with title + social proof]
```

### Cold Email (to practitioner/IC)
```
Subject: [Specific tool/process] at [Company]

Hi [Name],

Saw you're working on [specific project/initiative — from their LinkedIn, talks, blog].

We built [tool/solution] specifically for teams doing [what they do]. [Reference user] said it cut their [pain] by [metric].

Want me to set up a sandbox so you can kick the tires?
```
