# Lead Scoring Rubric

Score leads before spending time on personalized outreach. Focus effort on high-score prospects.

## Scoring Dimensions (1-5 each)

### Fit Score (does this prospect match your ICP?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Wrong industry, wrong size, wrong role |
| 2 | Right industry but wrong size or role |
| 3 | Right industry and size, unclear if decision maker |
| 4 | Matches ICP on industry, size, and role |
| 5 | Perfect ICP match + has budget authority |

### Intent Score (are they showing buying signals?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | No visible signals, cold as ice |
| 2 | General industry activity (posting, hiring) |
| 3 | Specific pain signals (complaints, reviews, gaps visible from outside) |
| 4 | Active solution seeking (posted about the problem, attended webinars, downloaded content) |
| 5 | Urgent trigger (funding round, executive change, competitor pressure, RFP posted) |

### Accessibility Score (can you actually reach them?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | No email, no LinkedIn, no public contact info |
| 2 | Generic company email only (info@) |
| 3 | LinkedIn profile, no direct email |
| 4 | Verified personal email or active LinkedIn with DMs open |
| 5 | Warm intro available, mutual connection, or they engaged with your content |

### Personalization Score (can you write a specific hook?)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Nothing to reference — no content, no news, no visible activity |
| 2 | Basic info only (company name, role) |
| 3 | Some content to reference (old blog post, company about page) |
| 4 | Recent activity to reference (LinkedIn post, news, launch, hire) |
| 5 | Strong hook (mutual connection, they engaged your content, shared problem publicly) |

## Priority Matrix

| Total Score | Priority | Action |
|-------------|----------|--------|
| 16-20 | 🔴 **Hot** | Personalize heavily, send first, follow up aggressively |
| 11-15 | 🟡 **Warm** | Personalize moderately, include in main sequence |
| 6-10 | 🔵 **Cool** | Light personalization, lower priority sequence |
| 4-5 | ⚪ **Cold** | Skip or batch into low-effort spray (if any) |

## Re-scoring Triggers

Re-score a lead when:
- They engage with your content (intent +1-2)
- They change jobs/roles (fit may change)
- Company announces funding/news (intent +1-2)
- They reply to your outreach, even negatively (accessibility +1)
- Time passes with no engagement after 2+ touches (intent -1)

## Integration with Outreach

- **Hot leads (16-20):** Use PAS or BAB framework. Reference specific trigger. Follow up within 3 days.
- **Warm leads (11-15):** Use AIDA framework. Personalize opening line. Standard 5-touch sequence.
- **Cool leads (6-10):** Use one-liner template. Minimal personalization. 3-touch max.
- **Cold leads (4-5):** Don't waste time. Re-evaluate in 30 days if new signals appear.
