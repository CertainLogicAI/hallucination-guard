# Demand Signal Tracker

Where to find evidence that a market opportunity is real. Evidence hierarchy: behavioral > stated > inferred.

## High-Confidence Signals (Behavioral)

People spending money or time = strong evidence.

| Signal | Where to Find | What It Proves |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **People paying competitors** | G2, Capterra revenue data, competitor pricing pages, Crunchbase funding | Willingness to pay exists |
| **Workaround spending** | Reddit, forums, job postings for manual roles | Pain is real, no good solution yet |
| **Job postings** | LinkedIn, Indeed — roles that your product replaces | Companies investing in solving this |
| **Freelancer demand** | Upwork, Fiverr — gigs related to the problem | People outsourcing because tools fail |
| **Pre-orders/waitlists** | ProductHunt launches, Kickstarter, landing page signups | Direct demand for the category |

## Medium-Confidence Signals (Stated)

People talking about the problem = decent evidence.

| Signal | Where to Find | What It Proves |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Pain discussions** | Reddit, Twitter, HackerNews, niche forums | Problem is acknowledged |
| **"How do you..." questions** | Quora, Stack Exchange, Reddit, Facebook groups | People seeking solutions |
| **Negative reviews** | G2, Capterra, App Store, Google Reviews of competitors | Current solutions inadequate |
| **Feature requests** | Competitor forums, GitHub issues, feedback boards | Unmet needs in existing products |
| **Conference talks** | YouTube, event agendas on the topic | Industry recognizes the problem |

## Lower-Confidence Signals (Inferred)

Indirect indicators = worth noting, not enough alone.

| Signal | Where to Find | What It Proves |
|--------|--------------|----------------|
| **Search volume** | Google Trends, Ahrefs, SEMrush | People looking for solutions |
| **Content volume** | Blog posts, YouTube videos on the topic | Market awareness growing |
| **Funding in space** | Crunchbase, TechCrunch, PitchBook | VCs believe in the market |
| **Regulatory changes** | Government sites, industry news | New requirements create demand |
| **Technology shifts** | HackerNews, tech blogs | Enabling technology creates opportunity |

## Evidence Grading

| Grade | Criteria | Examples |
|-------|----------|---------|
| **A** | Direct behavioral evidence from target customers | Pre-orders, competitor revenue, paid workarounds |
| **B** | Strong stated evidence from target customers | Repeated pain discussions, feature requests, negative reviews |
| **C** | Indirect or inferred evidence | Search trends, funding activity, conference mentions |
| **D** | Anecdotal or single-source | One person mentioned it, one blog post |
| **F** | No evidence, assumption only | "I think people would want this" |

**Rule:** Don't enter a market without at least 3 pieces of B-grade or higher evidence.
