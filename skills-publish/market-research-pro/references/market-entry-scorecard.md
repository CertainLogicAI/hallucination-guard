# Market Entry Decision Scorecard

Score each dimension 1-5. Use to decide: enter, defer, or skip a market.

## Dimensions

### Market Attractiveness (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Shrinking market, no growth, declining demand |
| 2 | Flat market, saturated, growth under 5%/year |
| 3 | Growing market (5-15%/year), moderate competition |
| 4 | Fast-growing (15-30%/year), clear demand expansion |
| 5 | Explosive growth (30%+), emerging category, land grab |

### Competitive Intensity (1-5, inverted: 5 = low competition)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Dominated by well-funded incumbents, high barriers |
| 2 | Several strong players, hard to differentiate |
| 3 | Moderate competition, room for differentiation |
| 4 | Fragmented market, no clear leader, opportunity to consolidate |
| 5 | Greenfield — few/no competitors, first-mover advantage |

### Customer Pain (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Mild annoyance, customers barely notice the problem |
| 2 | Noticeable pain but manageable with workarounds |
| 3 | Significant pain, customers actively seeking solutions |
| 4 | Urgent pain, customers spending real money/time on fixes |
| 5 | Hair-on-fire — customers desperate, willing to pay premium for solution |

### Our Fit (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | No relevant skills, assets, or network. Starting from zero. |
| 2 | Tangential experience, would need significant learning/hiring |
| 3 | Some relevant skills or assets, moderate ramp-up |
| 4 | Strong relevant experience, existing assets to leverage |
| 5 | Deep domain expertise, unfair advantage (network, tech, data, distribution) |

### Revenue Potential (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | Low willingness to pay, tiny market, sub-$10K/year potential |
| 2 | Small market or low prices, $10-50K/year potential |
| 3 | Moderate opportunity, $50-200K/year potential |
| 4 | Strong opportunity, $200K-1M/year potential |
| 5 | Large opportunity, $1M+/year potential |

### Speed to Revenue (1-5)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 1 | 12+ months to first dollar (heavy R&D, regulatory, etc.) |
| 2 | 6-12 months to revenue |
| 3 | 3-6 months to first paying customer |
| 4 | 1-3 months to revenue |
| 5 | Can generate revenue within weeks |

## Decision Matrix

| Total Score | Decision | Reasoning |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| 25-30 | 🟢 **Enter now** | Strong opportunity, move fast |
| 19-24 | 🟡 **Enter cautiously** | Good opportunity but manage risks |
| 13-18 | 🟠 **Defer** | Wait for better timing or more data |
| 6-12 | 🔴 **Skip** | Poor fit or unattractive market |
