CertainLogic Verifier has been deployed!

1. Get the service URL:
   export POD_NAME=$(kubectl get pods --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }} -l "app.kubernetes.io/name={{ include "hallucination-guard.name" . }},app.kubernetes.io/instance={{ .Release.Name }}" -o jsonpath="{.items[0].metadata.name}")
   kubectl port-forward $POD_NAME 8000:8000 --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}

2. Test validation:
   curl -X POST http://localhost:8000/validate \
     -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
     -d '{"query": "What is the price of GPT‑5?", "response": "$200/month"}'

3. View logs:
   kubectl logs -f $POD_NAME --namespace {{ .Release.Namespace }}

4. Provide your own facts_db.json:
   - Create a ConfigMap with your facts:
     kubectl create configmap my-facts --from-file=facts_db.json
   - Update the Helm values:
     factsDb:
       configMapName: "my-facts"
