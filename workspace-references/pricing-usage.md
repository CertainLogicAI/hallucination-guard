# Pricing Model — Pay-Per-Use (Credits)

Alternative to subscriptions: sell analysis **credits** that users consume as they go.

## Model

- User buys a credit pack (e.g., $20 = 200 credits)
- Each analysis costs X credits based on complexity:
  - Simple (no errors): 1 credit
  - Standard (some warnings): 2 credits
  - Complex (many issues): 5 credits
- Credits expire after 12 months (encourages usage)

## Advantages

- Lower barrier to entry (no recurring commitment)
-Fair billing — heavy users pay more
- Easy to add new products (different credit costs)
- Works well for infrequent users (industrial clients who analyze a few files monthly)

## Disadvantages

- Unpredictable revenue (spiky)
- Users may hoard credits or ration usage
- Requires metering + balance management

## Hybrid Approach

Offer both:
- Subscription (predictable revenue, included usage)
- Credit packs (top-ups for bursts)

Example: Pro tier ($49/mo) includes 100 credits; extra credits $0.10 each.
