# Pricing Model — Subscription Tiers

For SaaS products like FaultTrace, typical subscription tiers:

| Tier | Price | Included | Overages |
|------|-------|----------|----------|
| Hobbyist | $9/mo | 5 analyses | +$2/analysis |
| Pro | $49/mo | 100 analyses | +$0.50/analysis |
| Team | $99/mo | 500 analyses, 3 seats | +$0.25/analysis |
| Enterprise | Custom | Unlimited, SLA, on-prem | Negotiated |

## Pricing Principles

- **Tier usage** — Base number of API calls included; extra usage billed incrementally
- **Seat licensing** — Higher tiers allow multiple team members (API keys)
- **Commitment discounts** — Annual payment (10–15% off)
- **Enterprise** — Volume pricing, white-glove support, custom contracts

## Cost Structure Targets

At scale (12k analyses/mo):
- Hosting: ~$100
- Gross margin target: >80%
- Effective revenue per analysis: $0.04–$0.08 (blended)

## Psychology

- **Pro** is the "value anchor" — most users pick this
- **Team** priced for small companies wanting collaboration
- **Enterprise** is high-touch, high-margin
