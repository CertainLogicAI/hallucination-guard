What "Deterministic" Means

In our context, deterministic has two guarantees:

  • Operational determinism: The same query always returns the exact same answer (or the same refusal). No randomness, no surprises.
  • Factual determinism: Every non‑refusal answer is proven to contain at least three exact phrases from your approved reference documents. If it can't be traced back, it's blocked.

That's not just "low hallucination" — it's zero hallucination by design. The LLM may still generate falsehoods internally, but our guardrail catches them before they reach the user.
