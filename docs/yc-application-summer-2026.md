## What does your company do?

Every company has critical know-how scattered everywhere — in people's heads, Slack threads, old tickets, databases. Humans vaguely remember where that knowledge is and how to apply it. AI agents can't operate like that.

CertainLogic AI builds the Company Brain: a structured knowledge layer that captures how your company actually works, keeps it current, and turns it into executable automation with full audit trails.

AgentPathfinder is the first product — cryptographically-auditable task tracking for AI agents. We've already hit 185 downloads in 48 hours on ClawHub (2.3x the platform average), with zero paid marketing.

Our deterministic AI system (GBrain) stores facts, not guesses. When our auto-build system generates code, it queries the Company Brain first to ensure every build aligns with business requirements. Then it produces real, tested code — with HMAC-SHA256 verification on every step.

## What is going to be new about what you make?

Current solutions fall into two broken categories:
1. **Chatbots over documents** — ask questions, get answers, nothing changes
2. **Generic agent frameworks** — let LLMs loose with no guardrails

We're building the missing layer between raw company data and reliable AI automation:

- **Structured knowledge**, not search. Facts are HMAC-signed and deterministic. No hallucinations.
- **Executable output**, not chat. The Company Brain generates actual code, tests it, and audits every action.
- **Tamper-evident trails**. Every task, every build, every agent action is cryptographically verified. You can prove what happened.

Our subagent delegation system is unique: a parent agent can spawn a child agent to handle a complex implementation, track its progress in real-time, and verify its results before integration. No one else does this.

## What do you understand about your business that others don't?

The blocker for AI automation isn't the models anymore — it's domain knowledge capture. Companies think they need better prompts. What they actually need is better *context*. Their AI agents fail because they lack the company's actual operating knowledge.

We learned this the hard way. We built an "autonomous improvement" system that generated specs but couldn't execute — because it lacked structured context. We rebuilt around a fact database and now the system actually works.

The Company Brain isn't a chat interface. It's an infrastructure layer. Once it's populated, every agent in the company has the same ground truth.

- **LangSmith / Arize**: Performance monitoring for LLMs. They track what went wrong. We ensure it goes right.
