## The Archetype Name
**"The Reluctant Builder"**

Not the hype-chasing founder. Not the pitch-deck jockey. The person who:
- Builds first, talks later
- Gets annoyed at broken UX in tools they use
- Has strong opinions about frontend architecture but doesn't force them
- Would rather ship a working prototype than polish a keynote

---

## Quick Recognition (Conversation Signs)

### 🟢 GREEN FLAGS (30 seconds in)
| They say... | What it means |
|-------------|---------------|
| "The onboarding on [tool] is broken" | Cares about user experience, notices friction |
| "I built [thing] because I needed it" | Builder mentality, solves real problems |
| "I don't trust agents without audit trails" | Values determinism, understands risk |
| "I'd rather have 10 paying users than 1K free signups" | Revenue-focused, matches profitability ethos |
| "Documentation is the product" | Understands infrastructure marketing |
| "I worked on [devtools/SaaS] for 2+ years" | Has shipped, knows the long game |

### 🟡 YELLOW FLAGS (Ask follow-ups)
| They say... | Probe for... |
|-------------|-------------|
| "I'm between projects" | Did they ship or just experiment? |
| "I want to start something" | Do they have an idea or just want to founder? |
| "I can do frontend and backend" | Deep in one or mediocre in both? |
| "I know a lot about AI" | Do they mean prompt engineering or systems? |

### 🔴 RED FLAGS (End conversation politely)
| They say... | Why it's wrong |
|-------------|---------------|
| "Let's move fast" | Rushing bit us. Insta-no. |
| "Nobody reads docs" | We sell infrastructure. Docs ARE the product. |
| "We need to hire 5 engineers" | Pre-funding. No. |
| "LLMs will solve everything" | Doesn't understand deterministic layer value. |
| "Equity doesn't matter, I want salary" | Not aligned with pre-funding reality. |
| "I'll join if we get into YC" | Conditional commitment = conditional reliability. |

## The 15-Minute Test

**At a meetup, coffee chat, or event:**

**Minute 0-2:** Open with what CertainLogic does.
> "We make agents deterministic — cryptographic provenance, intent-based rules, audit trails. We're building the infrastructure that makes AI safe for small business."

**Watch their reaction:**
- 🟢 Eyes widen, lean in, ask about HMAC/signatures → **Hunter green flag**
- 🟡 Nods politely, asks about market size → **Yellow, probe deeper**
- 🔴 Eyes glaze, asks "what's your AI model?" → **Wrong person**

**Minute 3-8:** Ask about their work.
> "What's the last thing you shipped that you actually use?"

- 🟢 Shows a live URL, demo, or GitHub repo → **Green**
- 🟡 Describes something "almost done" → **Yellow**
- 🔴 Talks about ideas, no shipped work → **Red**

**Minute 9-12:** Share the co-founder need.
> "I'm looking for someone who owns the customer-side software layer — UX, onboarding, frontend. Someone who understands infrastructure IS the product. Equity-only until funded. Sound like anyone you know?"

- 🟢 "That sounds like me" or "I know exactly who that is" → **Green**
- 🟡 "Interesting, tell me more about salary" → **Yellow**
- 🔴 "You need how many engineers?" → **Red**

**Minute 13-15:** The brain capture test.
> "We have a hard rule: no public claims without documentation. No shipping without verification. Every action is auditable. Does that sound like bureaucracy or does it sound like safety?"

- 🟢 "That's the only way to build" → **Call them this week**
- 🟡 "I get it but flexibility matters" → **Yellow**
- 🔴 "That would slow us down" → **Never call**

## Physical Recognition (Optional)

Not required, but patterns in the best collaborators:
- Uses the tools they talk about (actual OpenClaw/Hermes installed)
- Has a messy GitHub with real commits, not just forks
- Carries a laptop to events, actually opens it to show something
- Asks "how does it work" before "what's the business model"
- References specific bugs or issues they've filed in projects

I need someone who can make it feel inevitable to a business owner who doesn't know what a hash function is. Equity. No salary until funded. If that sounds like you, let's build something real."

## Decision Shortcut

If you meet someone and you're not sure, ask one question:
> "Would you ship something publicly without testing it yourself first?"

- Yes without hesitation → **Red flag** (hasn't been burned by shipping bugs)
- "Depends on the context" → **Yellow** (situational ethics)
- "No, never. Documentation first." → **Green** (alignment confirmed)
